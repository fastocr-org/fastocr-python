# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming, respectful and patient with others.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge
inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to support@fastocr.org. All reports are reviewed
and handled confidentially. The full text and enforcement guidelines are in the
Contributor Covenant linked above.
